How to Request a Simba Cement Quotation in Kenya

Send your cement grade, quantity, project type and delivery location so the sales team has the information needed to prepare a clearer price and delivery quotation.

6 min read Quotation Guide Kenya
In this guide Quick quotation guide Details to provide Choose the cement grade Estimate your quantity Provide delivery information Review the quotation Sample WhatsApp request Frequently asked questions

What Do You Need to Request a Simba Cement Quote?

Send the cement grade, number of bags, project type and delivery location.

Add your preferred delivery date and site-access details for larger orders.

Cement Grade

State whether you need Simba Cement 32.5R, Simba Cement 42.5N or both.

Quantity

Provide the estimated number of 50kg bags required.

Project Location

Provide the town, county and exact delivery location where possible.

Project Type

Mention whether the cement is for a house, apartment, foundation, slab, masonry, block making or another project.

Details to Include in Your Quotation Request

  • Your name or company name
  • Simba Cement grade required
  • Number of 50kg bags
  • Construction project type
  • Delivery town
  • County
  • Exact site location where available
  • Preferred delivery date
  • Site-access information for bulk deliveries
  • Contact number
Better information gives a better quotation. A message saying "How much is cement?" does not contain enough detail for a useful delivery quote.

Step 1: Choose the Correct Simba Cement Grade

Identify Your Construction Work

Determine whether you are doing masonry, plastering, foundation concrete, columns, beams, slabs or several construction stages.

Select the Cement Grade

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general masonry, walling, mortar and plastering.

Simba Cement 42.5N is the higher-strength option for demanding structural concrete where specified.

Request Both Grades if Needed

A large project can require 42.5N for structural work and 32.5R for masonry or finishing.

Step 2: Estimate the Number of Cement Bags

Estimate your quantity before asking for a quotation.

Your estimate should be based on the actual construction stage rather than guessing.

House Construction

Break cement needs into foundations, slabs, columns, masonry, plastering and screeding.

Read: How Many Cement Bags Do I Need for a House? →

Foundation Construction

Calculate blinding, footing concrete, foundation walling, ground beams and floor slab separately.

Read: Cement Bags for Foundations →

Concrete Work

Calculate volume from length, width and thickness where possible.

Use the Concrete Volume Guide →

Step 3: Provide Your Delivery Location

Delivery location matters because transport requirements vary by distance, road access and order quantity.

Include:

  • Town
  • County
  • Estate, village or neighbourhood
  • Nearby landmark where useful
  • Map pin where available
  • Site contact person

Bulk Delivery Access

For large orders, mention whether the construction site has suitable access for a delivery truck.

Consider gate width, road condition, turning space and safe unloading area.

Read the Cement Site Delivery Checklist →

Step 4: Ask for Current Price and Delivery Terms

Cement prices change with market conditions, location, quantity and transport requirements.

Ask for:

  • Current price per 50kg bag
  • Bulk-order price where applicable
  • Total cost for your quantity
  • Delivery charge or delivery terms
  • Estimated delivery schedule
  • Payment terms
Do not rely on an old online cement price when placing a new order. Request the current quotation for your location and quantity.

Read: Simba Cement Prices in Kenya →

Step 5: Review the Quotation Before Ordering

Check the quotation carefully before confirming your order.

  • Correct Simba Cement grade
  • Correct number of bags
  • Correct 50kg bag specification
  • Unit price
  • Total cement cost
  • Delivery location
  • Transport charges or delivery terms
  • Expected delivery date or window
  • Payment details
Large order? Keep a copy of the quotation, invoice, payment record and delivery note for your project records.

Sample Simba Cement Quotation Request

Hello. I need a quotation for Simba Cement. Cement grade: Simba Cement 42.5N Quantity: 120 bags Project: House foundation and structural concrete Delivery location: Nakuru, Kenya Preferred delivery date: [insert date] Please send the current price per bag, total cost and delivery details. Thank you.

For masonry or plastering, replace the grade and project description with the correct details.

Hello. I need a quotation for Simba Cement 32.5R. Quantity: 80 bags Use: Walling and plastering Delivery location: [your town and county] Please send the current price and delivery information.

Requesting a Bulk Simba Cement Quote

For bulk orders, include more logistics information so the supplier understands your requirements before dispatch.

  • Number of bags
  • Cement grade
  • Construction stage
  • Delivery location
  • Site access
  • Preferred delivery date
  • Whether staged deliveries are required

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a Simba Cement quotation?

Send your required cement grade, number of bags, project type, delivery location and preferred delivery date through the available sales contact.

Can I request a quotation through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp sales button on this page and send your quantity, cement grade and delivery location.

What information should I include in a cement quote request?

Include the cement type, number of 50kg bags, project type, town, county and delivery requirements.

Should I ask for the price per bag or total price?

Ask for both. The quotation should make the unit price and total cost clear.

Does delivery location affect the quotation?

Yes. Transport arrangements depend on the project location, order size and site access.

Which Simba Cement should I request for plastering?

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general plastering, masonry and wall mortar.

Which Simba Cement should I request for columns and beams?

Simba Cement 42.5N is the higher-strength option for demanding structural concrete where specified.
