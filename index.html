Brand & Manufacturer Overview

Who is Simba Cement?

Simba Cement is Kenya's leading and most affordable construction cement brand, manufactured by the National Cement Company Limited, a subsidiary of the prominent Devki Group. Introduced to the Kenyan market in 2010, the brand is fully certified by the Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) and operates massive production plants in Athi River, Machakos County, and Salgaa, Nakuru County. It offers highly reliable compressive strength and setting times engineered for East Africa's tropical climate.

Buyer's Guide

Selecting the Right Cement for Your Project

When to Use Simba 32.5R (PPC)

Portland Pozzolana Cement (32.5R class) is formulated for non-structural masonry applications where workability, smooth finish, and thermal crack prevention are priorities.

  • Plastering & Rendering: Smooth finish with minimal shrinkage cracking.
  • Stone & Block Masonry: Excellent mortar bond strength.
  • Floor Screeds: Smooth wearing surfaces for residential floors.
  • Boundary Walls: Cost-effective masonry construction.
Read 32.5R Technical Guide

When to Use Simba 42.5N (OPC)

Ordinary Portland Cement (42.5N class) delivers rapid early strength gain and high structural load bearing capacity specified by structural engineers.

  • Suspended & Ground Slabs: Heavy load carrying capacity.
  • Reinforced Columns & Beams: Structural integrity for multi-storey frames.
  • Strip Foundations & Footings: Heavy foundation loads.
  • Precast Concrete & Culverts: High early demoulding strength.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Simba Cement

What is the price of Simba Cement in Kenya today?

Simba Cement 32.5R PPC is around KSh 550 per 50kg bag and Simba Cement 42.5N OPC is around KSh 680 per bag. Wholesale orders of 100+ bags qualify for factory-direct tiered pricing.

Which Simba Cement grade should I use for plastering?

Simba Cement 32.5R (PPC) is ideal for plastering, rendering, and mortar work because of its smooth workability, reduced heat of hydration, and crack resistance.

Which cement is recommended for structural slabs and foundations?

Use Simba Cement 42.5N (OPC) for structural concrete elements like floor slabs, columns, beams, and heavy footings according to structural engineer mix designs.

How fast is site delivery after placing an order?

Typical site delivery turnaround is 24 to 48 hours for Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Nakuru, Mombasa, and surrounding counties. Direct plant dispatches operate Monday through Saturday.

Is Simba Cement certified by KEBS?

Yes. Every batch of Simba Cement is manufactured to Kenya Bureau of Standards specification KS EAS 18-1 for composition, compressive strength, and soundness.
