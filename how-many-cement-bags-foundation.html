How Many Simba Cement Bags Are Needed for a Foundation?

A standard residential foundation in Kenya may require roughly 80 to 120 bags of 50kg cement as a broad planning estimate.

Actual usage depends on house size, trench depth and width, footing dimensions, foundation walling, concrete class and whether the estimate includes the ground floor slab.

A simple strip foundation for a compact house uses less cement than a larger foundation containing reinforced pad footings, columns, ground beams and a thicker concrete slab.

Structural quantities: Use drawings, concrete volumes and the engineer's specification for an accurate order. Bedroom count alone does not determine foundation cement requirements.

Foundation Cement Breakdown by Construction Stage

Blinding

5 to 10 bags

A broad reference for the thin levelling concrete layer placed beneath footings or other foundation elements.

Foundation Wall Mortar & DPC Work

15 to 25 bags

A planning range for foundation masonry mortar and associated general foundation walling work.

Strip Footings & Structural Concrete

30 to 45 bags

A rough reference for strip footing, pad footing or related structural foundation concrete.

Ground Floor Slab

40 to 54 bags

A planning reference for concrete floor slab casting, depending on area, thickness and mix specification.

Do not simply add every maximum figure and treat it as a guaranteed requirement. These are broad phase estimates. Actual quantities vary with the dimensions and construction method used.

Cement Bags for a 2-Bedroom House Foundation

For a modest two-bedroom residential project, around 100 to 120 bags is a broad planning reference where the estimate combines:

  • Foundation excavation-related concrete work
  • Blinding
  • Strip or pad footings
  • Foundation wall mortar
  • DPC-related masonry
  • Ground floor slab

The quantity rises when the foundation is deeper, the building footprint is larger or more reinforced concrete is required.

A compact house on straightforward ground can require less.

Blinding Cement Estimate

Blinding is a thin layer of lean concrete placed to create a clean working surface under structural foundation elements.

A rough estimate of 5 to 10 bags is useful for some modest residential foundations, but the actual amount depends on:

  • Blinding area
  • Thickness
  • Concrete mix
  • Footing layout

Foundation Wall Mortar Estimate

Around 15 to 25 bags is a broad planning range for foundation wall mortar and related masonry in the example used here.

The amount changes with:

  • Foundation perimeter
  • Number of internal foundation walls
  • Foundation wall height
  • Stone or block dimensions
  • Mortar joint thickness

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general masonry mortar.

Strip Footing and Foundation Concrete Estimate

Roughly 30 to 45 bags is a broad reference for structural footings and related foundation concrete in a modest residential project.

Actual quantity depends on:

  • Total footing length
  • Footing width
  • Footing depth
  • Number of pad footings
  • Ground beams
  • Required concrete class

Structural footing concrete should follow the approved structural design.

Ground Floor Slab Cement Estimate

Around 40 to 54 bags is a broad planning reference for a modest residential floor slab.

The required quantity depends on:

  • Floor area
  • Slab thickness
  • Concrete class
  • Mix design
  • Waste allowance

What Changes Your Foundation Cement Quantity?

  • Total house footprint
  • Number of bedrooms
  • Number of floors
  • Soil conditions
  • Foundation type
  • Trench depth
  • Trench width
  • Footing dimensions
  • Foundation wall height
  • Ground beam design
  • Number of structural columns
  • Floor slab area
  • Floor slab thickness
  • Concrete class
  • Mortar mix
  • Material wastage
Foundation design matters more than bedroom count. A two-bedroom house with deep reinforced foundations can use more cement than a larger house built on a simpler foundation system.

How to Calculate a Better Foundation Cement Estimate

For a more useful estimate, calculate each concrete element by volume.

For rectangular elements:

Concrete Volume = Length × Width × Depth

Calculate separate volumes for:

  • Strip footings
  • Pad footings
  • Ground beams
  • Foundation columns
  • Blinding
  • Floor slab

Then apply the specified concrete mix or design to estimate cement, sand and aggregate requirements.
