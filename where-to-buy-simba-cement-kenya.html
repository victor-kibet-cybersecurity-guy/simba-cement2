Where Can You Buy Simba Cement in Kenya?

Simba Cement is manufactured by National Cement Company Limited and distributed countrywide. Customers can purchase it through established cement distributors, hardware stores and bulk supply channels serving construction sites across Kenya.

National Cement Company lists Simba Cement 32.5R and Simba Cement 42.5N among its current cement products.

Its official website states that the company manufactures and distributes cement and paving blocks to customers countrywide.

For larger residential, commercial or infrastructure projects, requesting a delivered-site quotation is often more useful than comparing individual hardware-store prices.

National Cement Company Limited produces Simba Cement and operates its Kenyan cement manufacturing and distribution business from the Athi River area.

What to Check Before Buying Simba Cement

Inspect cement before accepting an order.

  • Confirm the Simba Cement product
  • Confirm 32.5R or 42.5N grade
  • Check the labelled bag weight
  • Inspect bags for moisture
  • Check for tears or damaged packaging
  • Feel for significant hard lumps
  • Confirm the number of bags
  • Check the delivery note
  • Move accepted bags into dry storage

Store Cement Properly After Buying

Purchasing good cement is only part of the process. Poor storage can damage bags before they are used.

Store cement:

  • Inside a dry covered building
  • On raised wooden pallets
  • Away from wet walls
  • Away from rain
  • In stable stacks
  • Using older suitable stock first
