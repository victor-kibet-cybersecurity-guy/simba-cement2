What Is Simba Cement 32.5R?

Simba Cement 32.5R is a rapid-hardening, general-purpose Portland Pozzolana cement produced by National Cement Company.

It is designed for a broad range of residential, masonry, finishing and light commercial construction applications.

The "32.5" identifies the cement strength class, while the "R" refers to rapid early strength development within the applicable cement classification.

Simba Cement 32.5R is commonly selected where builders need good workability, smooth spreading and reliable performance for general construction.

General-purpose cement: Simba Cement 32.5R fits applications such as walling, mortar, block laying, plastering, screeding and selected concrete works where the project specification permits this cement class.

Simba Cement 32.5R for Masonry and Block Laying

Masonry is one of the main applications for Simba Cement 32.5R.

It can be used in mortar for:

  • Natural building stone
  • Concrete blocks
  • Clay bricks
  • Partition walls
  • Boundary walls
  • General residential wall construction

Why It Works Well for Masonry

Masonry mortar requires workability so fundis can spread it consistently and form clean joints.

Simba Cement 32.5R provides a fine, workable cement system suited to this type of general construction.

Masonry tip: Use clean sand, consistent measured ratios and controlled water. Good cement does not compensate for dirty sand or excessive water.

What Does the "R" in Simba Cement 32.5R Mean?

The "R" indicates rapid early strength development within the relevant cement strength classification.

It does not mean the cement should be expected to dry instantly.

Mixing, setting, strength development and curing remain separate parts of the construction process.

Early strength does not remove the need for curing. Concrete, mortar and plaster still need suitable curing conditions for proper cement hydration and strength development.

Best Practices When Using Simba Cement 32.5R

  • Use clean sand and aggregates
  • Measure mix proportions consistently
  • Use clean water
  • Avoid excessive water
  • Dry blend materials before adding water
  • Mix manageable batches
  • Compact concrete properly where required
  • Cure mortar, concrete and blocks correctly
  • Store unused cement in a dry place

Storing Simba Cement 32.5R

Keep unopened bags inside a dry, enclosed storage room.

Raise cement above the floor on pallets and keep it away from damp walls.

Organize stock so older suitable bags are used first.

Check for wet packaging or significant hard lumps before use.
