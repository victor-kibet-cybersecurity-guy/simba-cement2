How Many Cement Bags Do I Need for a House in Kenya?

Estimate Simba Cement requirements for a one-bedroom, two-bedroom or three-bedroom house, then break the total into foundations, slabs, masonry, columns, beams, plastering and screeding.

Quick Cement Bag Estimates for House Construction

A standard three-bedroom house may require roughly 250 to 350 bags of 50kg cement as a broad planning estimate.

Smaller houses generally require fewer bags, while larger houses, reinforced concrete designs and multi-storey buildings require more.

1-Bedroom / Small House

80 to 120 bags

A rough estimate for a modest one-bedroom house or similar small residential project.

2-Bedroom House

150 to 220 bags

A broad planning range for a standard two-bedroom residential project.

3-Bedroom House

250 to 350 bags

A common planning range for a standard three-bedroom house, subject to design and specifications.

These are planning estimates, not bills of quantities.

Actual cement usage depends on floor area, foundation design, wall materials, number of floors, concrete classes, slab thickness, plaster area and structural design.

Cement Breakdown for a 3-Bedroom House

Instead of relying only on one total figure, divide your cement requirement into construction phases.

Construction Phase Approximate Bags Typical Use
Foundation 90 to 120 bags Blinding, strip footings, foundation concrete and related substructure work
Floor Slab 50 to 70 bags Ground floor concrete casting
Wall Masonry 30 to 45 bags Stone, brick or block-laying mortar
Columns, Beams and Ring Beam 35 to 50 bags Reinforced structural concrete
Plastering and Screeding 45 to 65 bags Internal and external walls plus floor screeding

Adding the upper end of every phase produces a larger total than some finished houses use. This happens because actual quantities overlap with design choices, floor area and specific construction methods.

Use the phase ranges for planning, then replace them with measurements from your drawings or bill of quantities.

Choosing the Right Simba Cement Type

A house can use both Simba Cement 32.5R and Simba Cement 42.5N at different stages.

Simba Cement 32.5R

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general construction work such as:

  • Stone masonry
  • Bricklaying
  • Block laying
  • Wall mortar
  • Plastering
  • General screeding

Simba Cement 42.5N

Simba Cement 42.5N is the higher-grade option for demanding structural concrete where specified.

  • Structural foundations
  • Heavy columns
  • Structural beams
  • Ring beams
  • Floor slabs
  • Roof slabs

Foundation Cement Estimate

Foundation work can consume a large percentage of the cement used in a house.

A rough three-bedroom estimate of around 90 to 120 bags can include:

  • Blinding concrete
  • Strip footings
  • Pad footings where applicable
  • Foundation concrete
  • Foundation masonry mortar
  • Ground beams where included

Foundation size changes significantly with soil conditions, house design and structural requirements.

Floor Slab Cement Estimate

A standard residential ground slab might consume around 50 to 70 bags within the broad three-bedroom estimate used in this guide.

Actual cement quantity depends on:

  • Floor area
  • Slab thickness
  • Specified concrete class
  • Mix design
  • Waste allowance

Masonry Cement Estimate

Around 30 to 45 bags is a rough reference for masonry mortar on a standard three-bedroom project.

The final figure depends on:

  • Wall length
  • Wall height
  • Stone, brick or block size
  • Number of internal partitions
  • Mortar joint thickness
  • Openings for doors and windows

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general masonry and wall mortar applications.

Columns, Beams and Ring Beam Cement Estimate

Roughly 35 to 50 bags can be used as a broad planning estimate for reinforced columns, beams and ring beams in a standard residential project.

The actual quantity depends on the structural drawings and concrete volume.

Simba Cement 42.5N is the higher-strength option for demanding reinforced concrete where the structural specification requires it.

Plastering and Screeding Cement Estimate

Around 45 to 65 bags is a useful broad reference for plastering and screeding in a three-bedroom house.

This varies with:

  • Internal wall area
  • External wall area
  • Ceiling or soffit plaster where applicable
  • Plaster thickness
  • Number of coats
  • Floor screed thickness

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general plastering and masonry finishing work.

What Changes the Number of Cement Bags Needed?

  • Total floor area
  • Number of bedrooms
  • Number of floors
  • Foundation depth and width
  • Soil and structural conditions
  • Wall material
  • Concrete slab thickness
  • Number and size of columns
  • Beam sizes
  • Plaster thickness
  • Concrete mix specification
  • Mortar mix specification
  • Material wastage
A three-bedroom house is not a standard engineering measurement.

Two three-bedroom houses can use very different cement quantities if one is 90 m² and another is 180 m², or if one has a simple roof while another has a reinforced concrete roof slab.

How to Plan Your Cement Order

Avoid ordering all estimated cement too early if your project will take several months.

A better approach is to divide the order into construction stages.

  • Foundation order
  • Slab and structural order
  • Masonry order
  • Plastering and finishing order

This approach reduces long-term cement storage and allows your quantities to become more accurate as construction progresses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cement bags do I need for a 3-bedroom house?

Roughly 250 to 350 bags of 50kg cement is a useful planning range for a standard three-bedroom house. Actual requirements depend on the drawings, foundation, floor area, structural concrete, masonry and finishing work.

How many cement bags do I need for a 2-bedroom house?

Around 150 to 220 bags is a rough planning estimate for a standard two-bedroom house.

How many cement bags do I need for a one-bedroom house?

Around 80 to 120 bags is a broad estimate for a modest one-bedroom or single-room residential project.

How many cement bags are needed for a 3-bedroom foundation?

Around 90 to 120 bags is a rough foundation-stage estimate, but actual quantities depend heavily on footing dimensions, ground beams, foundation masonry and structural design.

How many cement bags are needed for a floor slab?

Around 50 to 70 bags is a broad reference for the ground slab in the example three-bedroom house. Calculate from the actual slab volume and concrete specification for a better estimate.

Which Simba Cement should I use for masonry?

Simba Cement 32.5R is suited to general masonry, wall mortar, bricklaying and plastering.

Which Simba Cement should I use for columns and beams?

Simba Cement 42.5N is the higher-strength option for demanding structural concrete such as columns, beams and slabs where specified.

Should I buy all cement for my house at once?

Staged ordering is often more practical because it reduces long storage periods and allows quantities to be updated as the project progresses.
