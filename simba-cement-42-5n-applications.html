What Is Simba Cement 42.5N?

Simba Cement 42.5N, also known as Simba Power 42.5N, is a high-strength Ordinary Portland Cement manufactured by National Cement Company for specialized and demanding construction applications.
✓ KEBS Certified (KS EAS 18-1:2017) ✓ ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management 🏅 Structural Grade KEBS Mark

The product is designed for projects where structural performance, load-bearing capacity, durability and consistent concrete strength are important.

Simba Power 42.5N is used across reinforced concrete construction, infrastructure projects, high-rise developments, industrial facilities and precast concrete manufacturing.

The 42.5N designation identifies its cement strength class. The 42.5 classification relates to standardized 28-day compressive-strength performance, while the "N" identifies normal early strength development within the applicable cement standard.

Important strength distinction

The 42.5 designation is a cement strength classification. It does not mean every concrete mix made with the cement automatically achieves exactly 42.5 MPa. Final concrete strength depends on mix design, water-cement ratio, aggregates, batching, compaction and curing.

When Should You Choose Simba Power 42.5N?

Simba Power 42.5N is the stronger choice when your construction specification calls for a higher cement strength class and the project involves demanding reinforced concrete.

Typical examples include:

  • Load-bearing structural foundations
  • Reinforced concrete columns
  • Structural beams
  • Suspended floor slabs
  • Multi-storey buildings
  • Bridges and flyovers
  • Heavy-duty road works
  • Industrial floors and foundations
  • High-strength precast concrete
For structural work

Follow the structural engineer's concrete design, reinforcement details and specified material requirements. Cement strength class alone does not determine the final strength of a structural element.

When Is Simba Cement 32.5R More Appropriate?

A higher strength class is unnecessary for many routine finishing and masonry jobs.

Simba Cement 32.5R is better aligned with general applications such as:

  • Wall masonry
  • Brick laying
  • Block laying
  • Wall plastering
  • Rendering
  • Floor screeding
  • General residential construction

Getting Strong Concrete With Simba Power 42.5N

High-strength cement still requires correct site practices.

  • Use the concrete mix specified for the project
  • Measure cement, sand and aggregates accurately
  • Use clean construction water
  • Control the water-cement ratio
  • Use clean and properly graded aggregates
  • Compact concrete properly
  • Use vibration for reinforced structural concrete where appropriate
  • Protect fresh concrete from rapid moisture loss
  • Follow the specified curing procedure
  • Store unused cement in a dry location
